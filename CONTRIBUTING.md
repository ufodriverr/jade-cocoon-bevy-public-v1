# Contributing

Read [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md) first. Submit only work you have the right to license under
GNU GPL version 3 only (`GPL-3.0-only`). Contributions of original project work are
submitted under that licence. Identify third-party material and preserve its licence
and attribution; do not copy code from unlicensed projects.

Describe supported formats, interfaces and behaviour in your own words. Document
sources and authorship accurately. Do not claim a clean-room process or permission
from the original game's rights holders without evidence. Retain sensitive provenance
and lawful-access records privately; omission of internal notes is not legal clearance.

Never commit or attach game disc images, executables, BIOS files, extracted assets,
fonts, dialogue dumps, audio, movies, screenshots of game content, memory dumps or
disassembly/decompiler output. This applies to Git commits, issues, pull requests,
releases and CI artifacts. Use synthetic inputs for tests. Do not provide game-download
links, keys or protection-bypass instructions.

Keep local game content, configuration, saves, caches and captures out of packages.
Ignore rules are a precaution, not an audit of tracked files or Git history. Review
all files in a release explicitly rather than archiving your whole working directory.

Preserve required copyright and licence notices. Binary distributions must provide
their exact Corresponding Source, including required build/install scripts, using a
method allowed by GPLv3. Retain dependency notices and review licence compatibility.
See [BUILDING.md](BUILDING.md) for the build and release checks.

For bugs, describe steps to reproduce and include a short log without game-content
dumps or private paths. Send rights-holder concerns through the
[contact process](LEGAL.md#rights-holder-contact-and-removal).
